Ferrata vault: admin view

Reads a deployed FerrataVault (v5) through the JSON-RPC endpoint you type and shows its state, from the chain only. Read-only: it sends eth_chainId, eth_blockNumber, eth_call, eth_getLogs, eth_getBalance, eth_getCode and eth_getStorageAt to that endpoint and nothing anywhere else. It never signs or sends a transaction. Every read is pinned to one block.
