# Per-function review of the vault against the spec

Each row is one function of `FerrataVault` v5 ([`vault/src/FerrataVault.sol`](../vault/src/FerrataVault.sol), `FV`)
or of its linked library `FerrataRegistry` ([`vault/src/FerrataRegistry.sol`](../vault/src/FerrataRegistry.sol),
`FR`): the specification it implements, any deliberate deviation, what pins it, and the author's sign-off. Line
numbers are at `89e6d6d`. As in [REVIEW-core.md](REVIEW-core.md), the deviations column is part of the audit packet.

Specifications: `v2 §n` is [bridge-spec-v2.md](design/spec/bridge-spec-v2.md); `Dn` is an item of
[bridge-spec-v3-changes.md](design/spec/bridge-spec-v3-changes.md), which wins where the two differ;
`bridge.md §n` is [the bridge design note](design/bridge.md); `lez/BRIDGE.md §n` is
[the LEZ program's plan](../lez/BRIDGE.md), which defines the storage the LEZ side attests. "README" is
[`vault/README.md`](../vault/README.md), whose "Where I interpreted or departed from the spec" and "Known limits"
list the deviations at length; review ids (M-2, L-1, I-7, …) are those of
[the security review of 11 November](reviews/security/review-2026-11-11.md).

Pinned by: test names are in `vault/test/<Suite>.t.sol` (suite named where it is not obvious); `Vn` is mutant `n` of
`MUTANTS` in [`vault/tools/mutation_spotcheck.py`](../vault/tools/mutation_spotcheck.py) (0–253, all killed:
[EVIDENCE](EVIDENCE.md) row 12); `I:` an invariant of `test/invariant/VaultInvariants.t.sol`, run on a rule (A) and on
a rule (B) vault (`VaultInvariantsB`); `H:` a Halmos property of `test/halmos/VaultHalmos.t.sol` (EVIDENCE rows 35,
54: seven proven, `check_no_role_call_moves_funds` proven in the generic layout only, three not proven); `real/`
the suites that verify real Groth16 seals with RISC Zero's verifier (EVIDENCE rows 24, 41, 91). Gas: EVIDENCE rows
13 and 25; the deployment script: row 14.

Signed: not yet. Drafted on 12 November 2026 for the owner to read and sign, row by row, with the agent review of
12 November.
