# Security review: Ferrata bridge, branch `integration4` at `bc4a2a6`

11 November 2026. Adversarial review before the grant committee sees the code. Reviewer: Claude Code
(claude-opus-5-5), headless, read-only; nothing was fixed.

**Scope.** `vault/src` (FerrataVault v5, FerrataRegistry) and `vault/script/DeployVault.s.sol`;
`lez/guests/bridge` and `lez/bridge-wire`; `checkpoint/` (rules A and B, segments, key rotations, burn
collection, journal) and the release guest in `bridge/`; the attestation program `lez/guests/program`
with the gates of `crates/ferrata-core` it calls.

**Method.** The tree at `bc4a2a6` was extracted with `git archive` (no worktree, no build) and read
against `docs/design/spec/bridge-spec-v2.md`, `bridge-spec-v3-changes.md` (D1–D13),
`docs/design/bridge.md` and `docs/trust.md`. Where a finding depends on how LEZ executes, it was checked
against the LEZ v0.4.0-rc1 runtime source the guests are built against (`spike/upstream/lez-v0.4.0-rc1`,
the path in `lez/guests/bridge/Cargo.toml`). Nothing was executed: every finding is from reading, and the
attack steps are traced through the code with line numbers at `bc4a2a6`. The EVM checklist items of the
`audit` and `security` skills and their recorded model mistakes were applied; the pinned remote
checklists were not fetched (no network). **Not re-reported:** LEZ-side time taken from the caller
(being fixed on `lez-time-fix`).

## Summary

| ID | Severity | Where | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-1 | Critical | LEZ bridge program | Any claim can plant holder state at a bridge PDA that is not written yet: `Init` and `Register` can be blocked for good, and a single sequencer can destroy any pending claim (deposit lost for good) |
| H-1 | High | LEZ bridge program × checkpoint | `ImportRoot` is accepted from nested calls and asynchronous messages, which the checkpoint cannot see: after `Sunset` the refund boundary is too low and deposits are both claimed on LEZ and refunded on Ethereum |
| M-1 | Medium | Vault | Keyless retirement opens on inactivity: anyone ends deposits for good after 30 days without a finalisation (the first 30 days, a 30-day checkpoint freeze, a rogue watcher, a quiet month) |
| M-2 | Medium | Vault | The checkpoint fee goes to `msg.sender` of the finalisation: under rule (B) anyone takes it without a proof, under rule (A) by copying the seal; the prover is not paid |
| M-3 | Medium | Release guest × vault | The release nullifier is still SPEC-v2's index-dependent `NOut`; D13's double release after a revocation is open in the guest the vault pins |
| L-1 | Low | Vault | Stale candidates are never cleared and block their `newState` from the new `finalState` |
| L-2 | Low | Vault | Outbound fees come from the schedule in force at release, so a fee announced after a burn can be charged on it |
| L-3 | Low | Vault | Deposits are accepted after a finalised sunset checkpoint until `retire()`; they are neither claimable nor refundable until then |
| L-4 | Low | LEZ bridge program | The relay fee of a plaintext claim can be taken by copying the claim with another relayer |
| I-1 … I-10 | Informational | various | trust roots and deployment checks, deploy-script defaults, fee slippage, MAC v1, sequencer clock in the attestation program, instance halt by a third of a committee, `revokeFrom` bound, `relayer = 0`, future token program, D10 threshold |

**Counts:** critical 1, high 1, medium 3, low 4, informational 10; leads (not confirmed) 3.

## Critical

### C-1. A claim can plant holder state at any unwritten bridge PDA

**Where.**
- `lez/guests/bridge/src/main.rs:151-162`: `Mint` checks only that it comes from the nullifier actor
  named in the message; at a receiver with no state it creates `HolderState::default()` and credits it.
- `main.rs:629-666` (`spend`): mints the denomination to `mint_to` and each fee to its account
  (`:661-664`), with no restriction on the address.
- `main.rs:573-575` (`issue`): the relay fee goes to `claim.relayer`, chosen freely by whoever sends the
  claim; `main.rs:480` and `:530`: a public-kind destination is `witness.dest`, chosen by the depositor
  inside `C`.
- `lez/bridge-wire/src/lib.rs:556-558`: holder actors live at plain account ids of program B, the same
  address space as B's PDAs (`config`, `token`, `root`, `nullifier`, `burn`, `:534-553`).
- The write-once handlers then refuse for ever: `init` `main.rs:216-218`, `WriteToken` `:176-178`,
  `write_root` `:356-358`, `spend` `:643-645` (`ALREADY_CLAIMED`), the burn record `:688-690`;
  `token_state` `:86-92` and `ready_config` `:76-84` answer `BAD_STATE` to a holder state.
- LEZ: a program's own actor state is staged at any declared account whatever `is_authorized` says
  (`lee/state_machine/core/src/execution_state.rs:1431-1470`, `:1334-1339`), and a transaction can declare
  a public PDA of B with `PublicAccountEvidence::Pda { program: B, seed }`
  (`lee/state_machine/src/transaction_message.rs:16-19`). The seeds of `config`, `token` and `root` are
  public; a nullifier's needs `N`.

**Attack.**
1. Get one working claim in any configuration. The cheapest: a configuration of one's own. Deploy a
   small contract on Ethereum whose slots 0, 1, 2 and the registry and fee slots hold a tree of invented
   deposits, a non-zero relay fee and the attacker's own `cid`; `Init`, `Register` and `ImportRoot`
   against it through the honest attestation instance (the storage is real Ethereum state, so it
   attests). A real minimum deposit in the honest vault also works.
2. For a target address X (a bridge PDA with no state yet), send a claim whose public-kind `dest` is X
   and/or whose `relayer` is X (two targets per claim; the attacker's own configuration's fee recipient
   can be a third).
3. `Spend` sends `Mint` to `holder_actor(B, X)`, which writes a `Holder` state at X.

Targets:
- **(a) `config(cid)` before `Init`.** `cid` is public once the vault is deployed (slot 2). `Init` then
  fails for ever; the vault pins `lezConfigId` as an immutable, so the deployment is dead. A redeploy
  with a new salt can be squatted the same way.
- **(b) `token(cid, t)` before LEZ `Register`** (watch the vault's `TokenRegistered`, or squat ETH = 0x0,
  USDC, WETH in advance). `Register` fails for ever. Every deposit of `t` already in the vault is imported
  with the roots (imports do not look at tokens) but can never be claimed (`issue` → `BAD_STATE`), and,
  lying below the imported count, can never be refunded after a sunset: that escrow is stranded for good.
- **(c) `nullifier(chain, vault, N)` of a pending claim.** `N` is in the clear in the claim transaction
  (the plaintext claim; the `Issue` boundary delivery of a private claim). The sequencer that receives it
  (or anyone who sees it before inclusion) puts its own squatting claim first; the victim's claim fails
  with `ALREADY_CLAIMED` now and for ever. The deposit was imported, so it is not refundable either:
  **the victim's deposit is lost for good**.
- (d) `root(cid, n)` at future counts: imports at those counts fail (liveness; a later count works).
- (e) `burn(cid, H(note))` of a pending burn: that burn reverts (no loss; the wallet retries with a new
  tag); a censorship tool.

**Impact.** Funds: permanent freezing of a victim's deposit (c) and of every deposit of a squatted token
(b). Liveness: any new configuration can be kept from initialising (a). The attacker gains nothing
directly; it costs one contract on Ethereum and a few LEZ transactions, or the relay fee of one deposit.

**Preconditions.** Nobody corrupt for (a), (b), (d). For (c), one LEZ sequencer (or a public mempool):
this is a single-sequencer steering power of the kind the brief asked for.

**Test.** None. No harness test (`lez/harness/tests/bridge_*.rs`) names a PDA as `dest` or `relayer`. A
claim with `relayer = token_actor(cid, t).account_id` before `Register`, followed by `Register`, catches it.

**Smallest fix.** Never let a mint create state at an address B uses for anything else. Clean: make
holders PDAs of B under their own seed (`holder ‖ account`), so no holder address can equal any other
role's PDA; a burn is then a top-level call at the owner's own actor that forwards to its holder PDA with
the seed. Stopgap: in every write-once handler (`init`, `WriteToken`, `write_root`, `spend`, the burn
record) treat an existing `Holder` state as "not written" and overwrite it (the squatter loses what it
minted).

## High

### H-1. `ImportRoot` from a nested call or an asynchronous message is invisible to the checkpoint

**Where.**
- `lez/guests/bridge/src/main.rs:226-246` (`import_root`): no check of `input.from`. Compare `burn`
  `:710-714` and `role_message` `:760-763`, which refuse any call that carries a sender (D10).
- `checkpoint/crates/checkpoint-core/src/lez.rs:13-15` and `checkpoint/README.md:152-169`: calls made by a
  program inside a public execution and asynchronous messages are not in the block; "the bridge program
  must therefore act on `Burn`, `ImportRoot` and `Sunset` only when they reach it as a root call or a
  private→public boundary delivery". `rule.rs:57-63` collects only visible calls; `rule.rs:116-118` sets
  `importedLeafCount` to the largest *visible* import.
- Vault: the first sunset checkpoint sets `sunsetLeafCount = importedLeafCount`
  (`vault/src/FerrataVault.sol:822-827`); `refund` pays every leaf at or above it (`:696-722`).

**Attack.**
1. Use any LEZ program that forwards a sync message (LEZ's `scripted` test program does exactly this in
   `lez/harness/tests/bridge_burn.rs:226-241`), or an asynchronous message.
2. Each time the vault's leaf count grows, import the newest root through it. `config(cid)` attests slots
   0 and 1 and writes `root(cid, n)`; the block shows only a call to the attacker's program. An honest
   importer that tries the same count afterwards is refused (`ALREADY_WRITTEN`, a reverted transaction the
   checkpoint skips), so the checkpoint's `importedLeafCount` stays at the last visible import `m < n`.
3. Claim the attacker's leaves in `[m, n)` on LEZ against `root(cid, n)`.
4. The freeze authority sends `Sunset` (the routine migration step, bridge.md §16). The sunset checkpoint
   carries `importedLeafCount = m`; after `revokeWindow`, `refund` pays every leaf at or above `m` to its
   depositor.
5. The attacker's deposits in `[m, n)` were minted on LEZ and refunded on Ethereum. The wrapped tokens are
   unbacked; the shortfall falls on the last holders to release.

**Impact.** Theft of escrow equal to the attacker's deposits in the gap (any depositor in the gap can do
the same), bounded by the deposit caps, the LEZ mint caps and the release caps (refunds draw on the
release bucket). It breaks the D13 invariant "every leaf is claimable or refundable, never both".

**Preconditions.** No key. A forwarding program or an asynchronous message (lead LD-1); a `Sunset`, which
every migration needs; the attacker keeps its hidden imports ahead of honest importers until then.

**Test.** None for `ImportRoot`. The nested-call test exists for `Burn` (`bridge_burn.rs:226-241`) and was
not repeated for `ImportRoot`.

**Smallest fix.** In `import_root`, refuse unless `input.from.is_none()` (a top-level call or the root of
a private execution), the same line as in `burn`, so every executed import is a call the checkpoint sees.

## Medium

### M-1. Keyless retirement opens on inactivity, not on a lost key

**Where.** `vault/src/FerrataVault.sol:986-993` (`retire`), `:229` (`KEYLESS_RETIREMENT = 30 days`);
`lastFinalizedAt` is set at deployment (`:636`) and at each finalisation only (`:816`). A checkpoint
freeze lasts `maxFreeze` (`:966-969`, `:1473-1475`; 30 days by default); a watcher renews a 12-hour pause
at will (`:1007-1012`).

**Attack.** Anyone calls `retire()` once 30 days pass without a finalisation:
1. in the first 30 days after deployment if no checkpoint is finalised (no burns yet, so no fee pays a
   prover; M-2);
2. in any quiet month: provers are paid only through releases;
3. when the freeze authority's own checkpoint freeze for an incident runs its default 30 days: the clock
   kept running underneath it;
4. under one compromised watcher that pauses every 12 h and cancels every candidate, if the admin's
   14-day replacement is not started within 16 days.

**Impact.** Deposits end for good (retirement is one-way). Claims, burns and releases continue; refunds
need a LEZ `Sunset`, which only the freeze authority can send. Liveness, permanent, triggered by anyone.

**Preconditions.** A 30-day gap in finalisation, which needs no corruption.

**Test.** `vault/test/Roles.t.sol:240-271` tests the 30-day opening as intended; nothing tests the
interaction with a checkpoint freeze or a watcher pause.

**Smallest fix.** Measure from `max(lastFinalizedAt, checkpointsFrozenUntil)`, so time under a freeze or
pause does not count, and state in the operator guide that a checkpoint must be finalised at least every
30 days (a heartbeat), from deployment on.

### M-2. The checkpoint fee is credited to whoever sends the finalisation

**Where.** `FerrataVault.sol:810` (`finalizer: msg.sender`), `:1336`, `:1397-1404`. `finalizeAttested`
(`:785-793`) takes no proof; `CandidateSubmitted` (`:368-380`, emitted at `:1252-1266`) carries every
journal word except the immutables `nMin`, `tMin` and the zero `echoNonce`. Under rule (A), `finalize`
(`:770-780`) verifies a seal that is not bound to its sender.

**Attack.** Rule (B): rebuild the journal from the event and call `finalizeAttested` at
`submittedAt + W`: no proof, about 195k gas. The prover, who paid for the proof and `submitCandidate`
(about 348k gas), is credited nothing; the caller collects the checkpoint fee of every release against
that root. Rule (A): copy a pending `finalize` from the mempool and send it first.

**Impact.** The fee meant to pay provers (trust.md §7, bridge.md §13) goes to the fastest no-proof caller,
so the return leg relies on an operator proving at a loss; with M-1, a lapse of 30 days retires the vault.
No user funds lost.

**Preconditions.** None (rule B); a mempool observer or block builder (rule A).

**Test.** `vault/test/Fees.t.sol:588` and `:627` assert that the fee goes to the sender of the finalisation;
front-running is not modelled.

**Smallest fix.** Bind the beneficiary into the proven journal (a `prover` word the guest commits), so a
copied seal cannot redirect the credit; as a first step, store `msg.sender` of `submitCandidate` in
`Candidate` and credit it under rule (B) (`submitCandidate` itself can still be front-run without the
journal binding).

### M-3. The release guest still uses the index-dependent nullifier

**Where.** `bridge/crates/ferrata-bridge-core/src/outbound.rs:66-73` and `:185-189`:
`NOut = H("ferrata/nf-out/v1" ‖ sOut ‖ burnId)`, `burnId` includes `burnIndex`;
`bridge/guests/src/bin/release.rs` commits it and the vault pins that guest (`releaseImageId`). The vault
keys replay protection on `NOut` alone (`FerrataVault.sol:846`, `:1341`). D13 specifies
`NOut = H("ferrata/nf-out/v2" ‖ sOut)`; `lez/bridge-wire/src/lib.rs:1175` has it, but neither the guest
nor the CLI uses it (`cli/src/release.rs:78` checks the guest's v1 value). bridge.md §19 lists it as not
built.

**Attack.** A checkpoint is finalised over a LEZ branch that later loses (rule (A) outside F1–F4, or `t`
keys); releases are made against it after the release delay; the freeze authority revokes it inside
`revokeWindow` (used nullifiers stay used). On the canonical branch the same burn lands at another index
(other burns ordered first). The next honest checkpoint accepts a root with it at index `k' ≠ k`; the
holder proves again and gets a fresh `NOut`: one burn, two releases.

**Impact.** One extra denomination per re-indexed burn, bounded by the release caps; it defeats the
recovery that revocation is meant to give.

**Preconditions.** A wrong finalised checkpoint with releases before its revocation (itself a documented
loss case); this adds a second loss that revocation does not stop.

**Test.** None; the guest's vectors are SPEC-v2's.

**Smallest fix.** Move the guest, core and CLI to `NOut` v2 (a new release image id) before any
deployment relies on revocation.

## Low

### L-1. Stale candidates block their `newState`

**Where.** `FerrataVault.sol:743-747` refuses a submission while `candidates[newState].submittedAt != 0` in
the same revocation epoch; `_finalize` deletes only the finalised candidate (`:817`). `S` does not contain
the parent, so one state is reachable from several parents.

**Attack.** X submits `P → N` (tip 20). Y submits `P → N'` (tip 15) earlier; it finalises first. X's entry
for `N` stays live in the epoch, and `N' → N` (same tip, same state) is refused (`CandidateAlreadySubmitted`)
until a watcher or the freeze authority cancels it or a revocation happens. A griefer can do it on purpose
(each time at the cost of a valid proof).

**Impact.** Liveness: an honest prover re-proves to another tip. **Preconditions:** none.
**Test.** `vault/test/Checkpoint.t.sol:119-128` covers a live candidate only.
**Smallest fix.** Store the candidate's `parentState` and allow overwriting a candidate whose parent is not
`finalState`.

### L-2. Outbound fees are those in force at release

**Where.** `FerrataVault.sol:1397-1404` (`_feesInForce(token)` at release), `:853`; tested as intended in
`Fees.t.sol:556`.

**Scenario.** A user burns on LEZ (irreversible) while the burn and checkpoint fees are 0. The admin
announces 1% each right after. Wait `W` plus the release delay (up to `revokeWindow`, 7 days by default):
the release happens after the seven-day notice and pays the new fees.

**Impact.** Up to 2% of a denomination per in-flight burn; the schedule paid also narrows when the burn
was released. **Preconditions:** the admin. **Smallest fix:** charge the schedule in force when the root's
checkpoint was finalised (record the schedule index per checkpoint, as release delays already are), or the
lower of that and the current one.

### L-3. Deposits after a finalised sunset checkpoint

**Where.** `_checkDeposit` (`FerrataVault.sol:1163-1180`) checks `retired` but not `finalSunset`;
`refund` requires `retired` (`:704`).

**Scenario.** LEZ `Sunset` is sent and a sunset checkpoint finalises, but the vault is not retired yet
(two chains, possibly a lost freeze key; keyless retirement never opens while checkpoints keep
finalising). Deposits made meanwhile can never be imported and cannot be refunded until someone retires.

**Impact.** Funds locked until retirement. **Preconditions:** a slow or lost freeze authority.
**Smallest fix.** Refuse deposits once `finalSunset` is set, and let anyone `retire()` once a sunset
checkpoint has finalised (D11's first item, superseded by D13).

### L-4. Relay fee of a plaintext claim can be taken by a copier

**Where.** `lez/guests/bridge/src/main.rs:457-482` (`claim`: `relayer` is a free field beside the public
witness), `:573-575`.

**Attack.** Copy a pending plaintext claim with one's own account as `relayer` and get it included first
(a sequencer always can). The deposit still goes to the victim's `dest` (bound by `C`); the relay fee goes
to the copier; the original fails with `ALREADY_CLAIMED`.

**Impact.** The honest relayer loses at most 1% of a denomination; the user is unaffected. Private claims
are not affected (the relayer is inside the proof). **Smallest fix:** route the plaintext claim through
the relayer's own actor as a top-level call (as role messages are) and accept only `relayer == from`.

## Informational

- **I-1. Trust roots and deployment checks.** The vault's `genesisState` (the starting key set, turn length
  and tip) is a trust root on the level of the light client's checkpoint, but trust.md §1 does not list it.
  `DeployVault.s.sol` reads `genesisState`, `lezConfigId`, the image ids and `postingTimeframe` from a
  params file and checks none of them: not that `lezConfigId` binds the address the vault gets (D2), not
  `turnSeconds = posting_timeframe × slot` (D3, D10), not the image ids against reproducible builds. The
  constructor accepts `nMin = tMin = 1` (`FerrataVault.sol:576`), which turns on single-key support
  (`checkpoint-core/src/state.rs`, `allow_single_key`); "outside testnets `nMin ≥ 3`, `tMin ≥ 2`" is
  enforced nowhere.
- **I-2. Deploy-script defaults.** Freeze authority = a keyless address, no watchers, `revokeWindow` 1 day,
  `minReleaseDelay` 0, release delay 120 s, caps unlimited, release floor 0
  (`DeployVault.s.sol:184-227`, `:251`). The only guard is `chainid != 1` (`:136`), so it would deploy this on an L2
  mainnet. Right for a testnet; a production script with I-1's checks does not exist.
- **I-3. No maximum on an ERC-20 deposit.** If a fee change comes into force while a deposit is pending,
  `safeTransferFrom` pulls the new total (up to +1%) from an existing allowance (`FerrataVault.sol:675`,
  `:1178-1179`); the ETH path reverts instead (`:685`). Add a `maxTotal` argument.
- **I-4. MAC v1 in the wallet.** The core and CLI make and check `burn-mac/v1`, which does not cover
  `memo` (`outbound.rs`, `BurnNote::create`; D12's v2 is only in `bridge-wire`). A `kView`-only viewer can be
  shown a copied note with another memo (another recipient); the owner drops it on the `COut` check.
- **I-5. Sequencer clock in the attestation program.** `current_slot >= signature_slot` is enforced only
  through the transaction's validity window, i.e. the block time a sequencer chooses
  (`lez/guests/program/src/main.rs:195-209`, `:432-437`). Same class as the known time flaw; no impact
  found, since an update still needs a supermajority signature.
- **I-6. A third of one sync committee stops an instance, and the bridge with it.** Without force updates
  (`crates/ferrata-core/src/policy.rs:4`; `steps.rs:560-566`), 171 of 512 members withholding for a whole
  period leave the instance unable to move to the next committee. trust.md §2.2 says the instance "must be
  replaced"; the bridge pins the instance in `configId`, so this forces a full migration (`Sunset` is
  freeze-authority-only on LEZ). Cost to the minority: its sync rewards and penalties for about 27 h.
- **I-7. `revokeFrom`'s bound.** The loop is bounded by `revokeWindow / deltaFloor + 1`
  (`FerrataRegistry.sol:310-322`); the constructor accepts any `deltaFloor > 0`. With seconds-long floors
  under rule (B) a revocation of the oldest revocable checkpoint can exceed the block gas limit. Set a
  minimum floor.
- **I-8. `relayer = address(0)`** sends a release's relay fee to `msg.sender` (`FerrataVault.sol:850`):
  front-runnable; documented (bridge.md §12.3).
- **I-9. Future token program.** A registry entry's `lezDefinition` is attested from whatever vault a
  configuration names, so a configuration over a fake vault can name a real configuration's definition. If
  the mint authority becomes one PDA of B shared by all configurations (lez/BRIDGE.md §3), that
  configuration could mint the real wrapped token. Bind the mint authority per `(cid, token)`.
- **I-10. D10's rotation threshold.** D10 says the program checks the live `configuration_threshold`; the
  code checks `t(n)` signatures of the current keys and never compares `op.configuration_threshold`
  (`checkpoint-core/src/rule.rs:165-202`). Under V1 this gives `t` keys no new power, but a channel whose
  threshold falls below `t(n)` will have landed rotations the guest refuses (a stall).

## Leads (not confirmed)

- **LD-1.** Whether the live zone lets anyone deploy a program. H-1 needs a forwarding program or an
  asynchronous message; the harness shows the mechanics with LEZ's `scripted` program.
- **LD-2.** The checkpoint skips transactions in `BlockBody::reverted` and assumes that a
  privacy-preserving transaction whose public part fails is listed there (`checkpoint/README.md:161`). If
  LEZ ever settles such a transaction without listing it, its `PrivateToPublic` `Burn` delivery would be
  collected without a debit: a burn leaf with no burn. Not checked against LEZ's settlement code.
- **LD-3.** The BLS, SSZ and Merkle-Patricia internals of `ferrata-core` were not re-reviewed; only the
  gates the program applies (below).

## What was checked and found sound

**Vault (`FerrataVault.sol`, `FerrataRegistry.sol`).**
- Journals: the 17-word checkpoint journal and the 11-word release journal are built from `block.chainid`
  read at call time (no cached chain id), `address(this)` and the immutable `lezConfigId`; domain tags are
  distinct. Proofs reach only the immutable, stop-wrapped verifier; image ids are immutable, non-zero and
  distinct; `mode` is immutable and each finalisation path refuses the other mode; `finalizeAttested`
  checks the stop because it verifies nothing.
- Checkpoints: parent equals `finalState` at submission and finalisation; committee floors checked at
  submission and carried by the digest; the whole journal compared by digest at finalisation (tip, `n`,
  `t`, D11); the wait uses `deltaAtSubmit`; the rule (A) beacon is at least `W` after submission, one per
  block, nonce as specified; monotonicity of the imported count and sunset; `importedLeafCount ≤
  nextLeafIndex`; a revocation invalidates pending candidates; a live candidate cannot be resubmitted.
- Revocation: window check, refusal of a revoked index, restoration of the state, imported count and
  sunset flag, clearing of the sunset boundary; root acceptance through `burnRootCheckpoint`, re-accepted
  after a revocation; release delays per checkpoint index; D11's one-second boundary.
- Release: replay keyed on `NOut` alone; checks, proof, effects, then payments; `nonReentrant`; amount
  equals the registered denomination; relay fee 0 or registered; escrow and caps before payment; protocol
  fees credited, never pushed; failed payments become `owed` (`trySafeTransfer`; ETH call without copying
  return data).
- Refund: retired, not frozen, not stopped, leaf at or above the boundary, after the window, once per
  leaf, path direction bits equal to the index against the frozen root, the leaf's own fee schedule.
- Deposits: exact balance delta (fee-on-transfer refused), `SafeERC20`, exact ETH, plain ETH refused,
  tree full at 2^32, per-depositor index, leaf binds depositor, token, amount and `C`; caps.
- Accounting: `escrowed` grows by the whole total, shrinks by the denomination at release (fees split from
  it) and by the whole escrow at refund; `totalOwed` tracked; no other path moves tokens; no upgrade, no
  rescue, no `delegatecall` except the linked library, whose deployed code the deploy script checks.
- Roles and bounds: admin cannot freeze, cancel, revoke or retire; freeze authority cannot touch the
  registry, caps or escrow; two-step admin; 14-day role proposal; roles kept distinct; watchers limited to
  cancel and a 12-hour pause; `delta` raise-only and ≤ 30 days; delays within
  `[minReleaseDelay, revokeWindow]`; release-cap floor in value units; registry write-once with its bounds;
  fee ceilings; published slots and hashes equal bridge-wire's encodings; bucket arithmetic cannot
  overflow; storage slots 0, 1, 2 are the interface (the guard uses transient storage).
- EVM checklist items: `block.number` only de-duplicates beacons (not a clock); no signatures or EIP-712;
  loops bounded (32 levels, ≤ 8 denominations, binary searches; `revokeFrom` see I-7); every
  value-moving entry point follows checks-effects-interactions; no bare ERC-20 calls; no oracle, AMM or
  share accounting.

**LEZ bridge program (`lez/guests/bridge`, `lez/bridge-wire`).**
- `configId` binds Bedrock genesis, channel, header, image, chain, vault, attestation program and instance
  and the roles; the reciprocal pairing allows one configuration per vault.
- A visible `ImportRoot` writes exactly the attested root and count of one record (hops 0, instance, chain
  and slot checked), once per count, never after `Sunset`.
- Claims: kind, `leafIndex < count` and `< 2^32`, the fee-schedule range against the attested schedules
  and `known_until`, path direction bits, opening of `C`, `N` with chain and vault from the root; one
  nullifier namespace `(chain, vault, N)`; a private-kind claim must run as `dest`; replies are accepted
  only from the actor awaited, and every attestation path either replies or fails, so `config(cid)` cannot
  be left pending.
- Burns: D10 (no sender, authorised), debit before the note, the token admits only actors of B, the
  record only the token actor, the note write-once over all its fields, registered u128 amounts. `Sunset`,
  `Freeze`, `SetCap` only through the role's own actor from a top-level call; the admin cannot unfreeze;
  the cap floor holds.
- Sender spoofing: LEZ's blinded aliases are hashes, private PDAs use another prefix, and an alias that
  collides with a declared account is refused, so no private actor can present a bridge PDA's address
  (checked in the LEZ source).

**Checkpoint program (`checkpoint/`).**
- Blocks parsed strictly, re-hashed, linked by id and previous hash; every visible call to the bridge in a
  non-reverted transaction applied in order; other configurations ignored; the burn leaf binds
  configuration and index; the frontier is canonical; the state digest is recomputed from keys, turn
  length and frontier, so a prover cannot supply inconsistent parts; segments are verified with
  `env::verify` against an image id fixed at build time and must chain; at least one new block.
- Rotations: channel, parent and turn length pinned (non-zero), keys valid and not weak, distinct
  signature indices, `t(n)` signatures over the Mantle transaction hash, distinct keys and both floors for
  every set reached; single-key support tied to `nMin`.
- Rule (A): the producer's inscription checked and its signer in `K`; support strictly after `Y`, distinct
  keys of `K` other than `D`; inscription framing canonical with a closed opcode set, channel and payload
  length pinned, `verify_strict` over the whole transaction hash.
- Rule (B): the attested tip is `T` or a descendant on the linked path; the channel key is in `K`; the
  certificate binds chain, vault, configuration and channel; the attestation adds `keySetHash(K)`, the tip
  and the message id; no channel or attestation key repeats; `t(n)` entries; `verify_strict`.
- What a prover can still choose is the documented set: the tip, a stale key set (V1; watchers cancel
  under (A), attestors' live key set refuses it under (B)), configuration branches (LEMMA §5.6). The one
  gap found is H-1, which is the bridge program's side of the contract.

**Release guest (`bridge/`).** `COut` binds chain, vault and recipient; the leaf binds configuration,
index, token, amount, `COut` and `r`; direction bits equal the index (a `u32`, so below 2^32); the journal
binds every public field; the vault bounds the relay fee. Witness-independent cycle count: CI gate, not
re-run here. Nullifier: M-3.

**Attestation program (`lez/guests/program`).** Updates are finality updates with at least two thirds
participation (`steps.rs:560-566`); a job is applied only to the store it started from (`steps.rs:605-624`);
the private placement accepts `ApplyVerified` only from its own program and never an activation; records
are written once, only by their instance, and a conflicting record is refused; a Gloas record needs the
RLP header that hashes to the recorded block hash; the bridge asks for no hops and checks the instance and
chain. A malicious submitter can only spend its own fees (jobs it never finishes, unrelated instances); a
minority below two thirds of a committee gets nothing accepted; a third or more can stall an instance
(I-6).
