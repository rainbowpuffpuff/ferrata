# ADR 0021: Fees bound in proofs, schedules announced seven days ahead, each at most 1%; the checkpoint fee pays provers

- **Date:** 9 November 2026 (SPEC-v2: the 1% ceiling and the 7-day notice); fee collection and the checkpoint fee 10 November 2026 (D13)
- **Status:** accepted; built
- **Requirements:** RFP-022 F.13, Sec.3

## Context

Relayers and provers need payment without being able to overcharge or redirect; users need to know the fee before they
deposit.

## Options considered

1. Fees set by relayers per transaction. *Reconstructed for contrast* (not recorded as an option at the time).
2. A registered relay fee bound inside the claim and release proofs; mint and burn fees in schedules announced seven
   days ahead, a deposit keeping its leaf's schedule; a checkpoint fee credited to the candidate's prover; every fee
   under an immutable 1% ceiling.

## Decision

The second.

## Rationale and trade-offs

No relayer can inflate its fee or redirect funds; provers are paid per release. One exception: a plaintext claim
(the user's explicit choice) does not bind its relayer, so whoever gets it included first takes its relay fee
(docs/trust.md §7, security review L-4, not changed). The cost: a schedule change splits
anonymity sets by date (`docs/trust.md` §8). Whether fees cover the callers' gas is not measured.
