Check it, don't trust it

Everything this site claims can be checked from your own machine. These pages say what to check and how, and where a check stops.

  • Bridge security

    What the bridge trusts, who holds which power, the caps and freezes, RISC Zero's advisory and our correction of our own note, and cast calls to read the vault yourself.

  • Bridge privacy

    What stays on your machine (measured: 320 secrets searched for in every public byte, none found), what is public by design, what still correlates, and what this host logs.

  • Verify this site

    Every served file's SHA-256 and commit, in a manifest the repository also holds; a script anyone runs compares the host's copy, the repository's copy and the served bytes. Most of those commits are not public yet; the manifest says which.

  • Apps and programs

    Every deployed program rebuilt from its source commit to the same image id, compared live with the loader header on chain (all nine from public commits); every app's files hashed against the manifest.

  • Accessibility

    axe-core and keyboard checks of every page in two browser engines, with what fails, who fixes it, and what automated checks cannot tell.

What these checks do not cover

  • A host can serve different files to different visitors; a check shows what you were served. Run the wallet from your clone if that matters to you.
  • Server programs (RPC gateway, faucet, updater) are public source, but what runs on the host cannot be proven from outside; what the chain records can, from your own node.
  • Nothing here is audited by a third party. The test network carries test value only.

This page is static and its source is in the public repository: deploy/testnet/web/trust/. Every file this host serves is listed with its SHA-256 and commit in the site manifest; check it from a clone with python3 -I scripts/site-manifest.py verify. No cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts.
