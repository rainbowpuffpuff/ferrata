A wallet for LEZ, in this page

Your keys are made and kept in this browser, encrypted with your passphrase. They are never sent anywhere: the page talks only to the network's own RPC, and signs every transaction itself.

Test network only: its coins have no value. Prototype, unaudited.

Your recovery phrase

These 24 words are your wallet. Anyone who has them controls it; without them, a lost passphrase cannot be recovered. The phrase is the same kind LEZ's own wallet uses (BIP-39, LEZ's key tree), so it also opens your accounts there.
