What “shown” means here: strict on purpose

The status of every row of traceability.md: 64 rows for RFP-021 and 73 for RFP-022. The classification is the project's own, made on 18 November 2026 and updated on 25 November 2026 from the matrix as it stands at this commit, and it is strict on purpose. A row is shown only if every clause of the requirement, as the RFP words it, is demonstrated today by a named test or a recorded run on real code: LEZ v0.4 release candidates' own state machine (the in-process harness) or one of their sequencers for the LEZ side, a local EVM or Sepolia for the Ethereum side. Proofs in RISC Zero dev mode count only where the requirement is not about proving. A row is in part when a clause is demonstrated and another is not, when the behaviour is narrower than the RFP asks, when the only run is in the native core or in an in-process stand-in for a component the requirement names, or when a required document exists only as a first draft. not yet means no clause is demonstrated, or the row is neither offered nor built. waits on Logos means the requirement names LEZ testnet 0.4 or another upstream component that is not released; the reason says what is shown instead. A trust assumption or a missing upstream library that the matrix states as a condition is given in the reason and does not by itself lower a status. Where a choice was open, the weaker status was taken. Every sequencer named here is one we ran; none is a LEZ network Logos runs (LEZ testnet 0.4 does not exist). One of ours is reachable publicly: a LEZ v0.4.0-rc3 network with its own Logos Blockchain node, named where it is used. What it is

What
A LEZ program checks Ethereum's sync-committee signatures itself and keeps a write-once record of each finalized header; any LEZ program proves an account, a storage slot or a log against it. The bridge escrows ERC-20s and ETH in an Ethereum vault and mints on LEZ against an attested deposit; the vault releases against a LEZ burn proven to it.
Answers
RFP-021 and RFP-022, one codebase, tested against each other end to end.
Proposal
logos-co/rfp #257, filed 2026-11-15; open (as read 2026-11-23).
Ask
The bid is $65,000. $50,000 pays for 12 weeks of remaining work: a one-week check of what is still open, 4 weeks for RFP-021, then 6 for RFP-022 (these 11 in 14 calendar weeks), and a week of audit remediation after the reports. $15,000 goes toward the vault's third-party review (§A8). Only unbuilt work is charged, not what the table above shows. (the proposal)
Platform
LEZ v0.4.0-rc1 to rc3 on zones we run; LEZ testnet 0.4 is not released; the Ethereum side on Sepolia and local EVMs.

Security note, 21 November 2026: RISC Zero's Groth16 advisory and Ferrata

Corrected 23 November 2026: the light client's public path is not independent of LEZ's check of privacy-preserving transactions, as "Not affected" below said; see the correction. The rest of the note stands as written on 21 November 2026, except that its "no live exposure" sentence stopped holding later on 23 November 2026, when the project's public test network started running the v0.4 programs: see "Corrected again" in the correction.

RISC Zero published two critical advisories on 11 November 2026: GHSA-g6qg-9j8p-7j87 (risc0-zkvm) and GHSA-mm6c-35cm-g4x2 (the Ethereum verifier contracts). Their words: the Groth16 verifier "accepts a Groth16 receipt whose claim digest has been substituted with the digest of a different, attacker-chosen ReceiptClaim. An attacker who can produce one valid Groth16 receipt can therefore have an arbitrary claim, including its image ID and journal, accepted". Every RiscZeroGroth16Verifier of version 3.0.0 or earlier accepts the forged proofs; risc0-zkvm before 3.1.0, risc0-circuit-recursion before 4.1.0 and risc0-groth16 before 3.0.6 are affected. The fix is risc0-zkvm 3.1.0 with a new trusted setup and verifier 3.1.0 (selector 0xe71e6620); receipts made by earlier versions do not verify under 3.1.0. RISC Zero emergency-stopped its own deployments of verifier 3.0.0 (0x73c457ba) and earlier, and writes: "Applications that call a RiscZeroGroth16Verifier address directly must switch to the 3.1.0 verifier."

Everything in this repository that proves is built on risc0-zkvm 3.0.5, and every Groth16 seal it records carries the selector 0x73c457ba. Links to both advisories were in public material this project saved on 19 November 2026; the project did not act on them until a code review raised them on 21 November 2026.

The correction of 23 November 2026, as written

What the note said. Under "Not affected" (above, as written on 21 November 2026): "LEZ public transactions, which the sequencer executes rather than verifies by proof (the light client's updates and records on its public path, …)".

What holds. A public transaction is executed by the sequencer, not checked by proof, so the public path's own steps (the job's Open, Miller and Finish, and Attest) do not depend on RISC Zero. The state they read does. On LEZ v0.4 a privacy-preserving transaction acts on public actors through the calls its journal lists, and LEZ replays each on the public side as the journal states it, sender, inherited authorizations and PDA seeds included, with no check of its own (LEZ's lee/state_machine/core/src/execution_state.rs:842-872, proven.with_from(Sender::Call(proven.envelope.from)) at :849, at v0.4.0-rc1 and rc3); a receiving program cannot tell such a call from a call another actor makes (ReceiveInput, lee/state_machine/core/src/program/mod.rs:373-385), though its own checks of the call's content still run (execution_state.rs:910-917). Only the receipt check binds these calls to a real private run. LEZ v0.4.0-rc1 and rc3 accept a transaction when its proof, decoded as any InnerReceipt, passes Receipt::verify for the privacy circuit (lee/state_machine/src/privacy_preserving_transaction/circuit/mod.rs:51-65, the same in both). With the versions both lock (risc0-zkvm 3.0.5, risc0-groth16 3.0.4, risc0-circuit-recursion 4.0.4), that accepts a Groth16 receipt as well as a succinct one (risc0-zkvm 3.0.5 src/receipt.rs:344-354): the receipt kind GHSA-g6qg-9j8p-7j87 is about, in its affected versions. So if LEZ accepts a forged receipt, which §3 above leaves to Logos to confirm and which we have not tested (we built no forgery), it could, by our reading of LEZ's source, write any instance's store and records: through ApplyVerified, which an instance accepts from any actor of the program (lez/guests/program/src/main.rs:288-305), or through Apply and Record sent in a job's or the instance's name (:269-286, :405-425). A job is applied after a check of its phase, its adoption and its starting store (crates/ferrata-core/src/steps.rs:605-623) and the period rules of apply_core_compact (:304-335). The records public consumers read follow from that store. GHSA-mm6c-35cm-g4x2 (the Solidity verifiers) does not reach LEZ. Every Ferrata program's public state rests on the same check: the bridge program's (issue and the burn and transfer deliveries, lez/guests/bridge/src/main.rs:639, :775, :852, among others), the reference consumer's (lez/guests/consumer/src/main.rs:185), and that of the programs that accept a reply by its sender (ferrata_holdings, ferrata_statements, the statements reader, ferrata_rebate), as docs/trust.md §5 lists. RFP-022's rows Sec.2, Sec.3 and Sec.6 state it for the bridge.
