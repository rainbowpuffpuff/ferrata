# Security review of 11 November 2026: what was fixed, what was not, and why

The review (`review-2026-11-11.md`, this folder) is an adversarial reading of `integration4` at `1bb20d5` by a coding
agent (Claude Code), read-only, from the source: not an audit. It found 1 critical, 1 high, 3 medium, 4 low and
10 informational issues. Ten are fixed, each with a test that fails on `1bb20d5` and passes after the fix; nine are
not fixed, each for the reason below. The fixes were made on the branches `secfix-lez` (fixes and tests: e065f96) and
`secfix-vault` (fff6ec5; M-3's vault-side test c9d67fc) and merged into `integration5` (4b89b42, d65c369).

Measured on the fix branches (build box, dev mode for LEZ): the LEZ harness 44 of 44 (36 before, 8 new), the bridge
core 56 of 56, the vault 422 passed and 1 skipped, 100% line and branch coverage, 254 of 254 mutants killed. Costs:
a public burn 130,868 → 158,905 gas (one more handler), a plaintext claim 239,431 → 252,941, `ImportRoot` 226,091 →
225,793 (`lez/BRIDGE-RESULTS.md` §13; the fix branch alone, superseded: merged with the time fix, the same rows are
164,153, 258,885 and 225,898 in `lez/results/`); on the vault +2.1k gas per deposit, +24.65k per `submitCandidate`, +3.3k per
finalisation, nothing per release (`vault/GAS.md`).

Not re-reported by the review, and fixed on `lez-time-fix` (merged in `integration5`, bf2328e): the LEZ bridge
program took its clock from the caller. It now takes it from the newest attested Ethereum record of its own instance
(`lez/BRIDGE.md` §6; `lez/harness/tests/bridge_time.rs`).
