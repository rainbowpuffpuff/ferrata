# State-growth projections (RFP-021 P.7, RFP-022 P.7)

Generated by `python3 -I bench/perf/projections.py` from measured inputs only: account sizes and growth per
record in LEZ v0.4.0-rc1's serialized state ([`perf-state.md`](perf-state.md)), the records the lab wrote in
operation ([`perf-tracking.md`](perf-tracking.md)), the vault slots each Ethereum operation creates
([`perf-state-ethereum.txt`](perf-state-ethereum.txt)). Each projection is *derived*: a measured size times a
count. LEZ v0.4 charges storage gas on a transaction's bytes, not on the state it leaves: the growth is disk on
every node, not a fee.

## RFP-021 P.7: record state per instance, one and five years

A record is **453 B** in the serialized state (its 32-byte key and its `Account`); measured growth 453.00 B per record from 0 to 100,000 records. The instance (593 B) is constant; a job's account (1,110 B) is cleared by `Finish`.

| cadence | records a year | one year | five years | basis of the count |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| as the lab ran (one update every two hours, each period's) | 4,804 | 2.18 MB | 10.88 MB | measured: 106 jobs in 8.06 days on mainnet (9 of them period updates) |
| the design's: one an hour while roots are imported, plus each period's | 9,087 | 4.12 MB | 20.58 MB | 8,766 hourly updates and 321 period updates a year |
| every finalized epoch (6.4 min), as the live Sepolia daemon ran | 82,181 | 37.23 MB | 186.14 MB | an update per epoch |

A job that never finishes leaves its account (1,110 B; clean-up is not built). With k operators racing at the design's cadence, at most one abandoned job per extra operator per update (9,087 a year): (k − 1) × 10.09 MB a year and (k − 1) × 50.43 MB in five years; none with one operator.

## RFP-022 P.7: bridge state at 1M and 10M operations

Measured per record: a nullifier 165 B, a burn record 273 B (LEZ's serialized state, key included); Ethereum: a release creates 1 vault slot (25 for 25), a new depositor's first deposit 1 (28 for 25 new depositors: one each and 3 as the deposit tree's frontier took new levels; the frontier is 32 slots at most, ever), a repeat deposit none (1 for 25: a frontier level).

| operations | LEZ: nullifier records (claims) | LEZ: burn records (burns) | Ethereum: vault slots for releases | Ethereum: vault slots for deposits, every depositor new / all repeat |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1,000,000 | 165.00 MB | 273.00 MB | 1,000,000 (64.00 MB of keys and values) | 1,000,000 + at most 32 / at most 32 |
| 10,000,000 | 1.65 GB | 2.73 GB | 10,000,000 (640.00 MB of keys and values) | 10,000,000 + at most 32 / at most 32 |

Roots grow with the import cadence, not with deposits: 205 B each (173 B `Account` and its key), 8,766 a year at one an hour: 1.80 MB a year. Ethereum slot bytes are counted as a 32-byte key and a 32-byte value (the trie's own overhead is the client's).
