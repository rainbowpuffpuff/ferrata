# The guest's phases for placements of 170 absent members that all need 425 hashes and 256 witness nodes

Written by `cargo test --release -p residue-host --test placements` (bench/residue-host/tests/placements.rs;
bench/RESIDUE.md §9.6). The whole 342-signer finality update in the bench guest guests/residue-agg-accel (r0vm
executor), the witness check, the non-signers as one multiproof summed by blst's batch addition. User cycles per
phase, as `residue-host measure-agg` prints them (results/residue/agg/one-tx.txt). Experimental and unaudited.
