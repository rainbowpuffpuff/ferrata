Ferrata web depositor

One static page that sends a Ferrata bridge deposit from your own Ethereum wallet (anything that injects window.ethereum: MetaMask, Rabby, …), so funds do not have to sit in Basecamp first. It makes no request of its own — no fetch, no external script, font or image — and reaches the chain only through the wallet. Deposits only: claiming stays in your Logos wallet (the tool or Basecamp), because the browser must never hold your LEZ seed. The deposit ticket (the commitment C and its index j) comes from your own Logos tool; the seed never enters this page.

1. The wallet

No wallet connected yet. The page will ask it only for: eth_requestAccounts, eth_accounts, eth_chainId, eth_call, eth_getCode, eth_getBlockByNumber, eth_blockNumber, eth_sendTransaction, eth_getTransactionReceipt — and refuses every other method.

2. The deposit ticket from your Logos tool

Make it with a dry run of the Logos tool (it sends nothing): ferrata deposit --from <your wallet address> --denomination N. It prints the commitment C and j; the vault and the chain are on its first line. Paste the ticket as JSON, or type the fields below. For native ETH the token is the vault's ETH constant, 0x0000…0000.

{"version":1,"chainId":11155111,"vault":"0x…","token":"0x…","denomIndex":0, "commitment":"0x… 32 bytes","depositor":"0x…","index":0}
